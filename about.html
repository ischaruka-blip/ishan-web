The PG Creation Story

Founded in 2023 by Ishan Charuka, PG Creation began with a clear purpose: to bridge the gap between high-level creative aesthetics and the demanding requirements of professional typesetting.

Having studied in the Physical Science / Mathematics stream for G.C.E. Advanced Level (Combined Maths, Physics, and Chemistry) alongside achieving 8 'A' passes at Ordinary Level (including Mathematics, Science, Art, and ICT), Ishan brings a unique blend of mathematical logic, strict typographic accuracy, and artistic flair to every single project.

From formatting comprehensive examination papers and syllabus modules for educators and tuition masters, to designing high-converting social media creatives and managing Meta ad boosting campaigns—every deliverable is crafted with utmost discipline, speed, and client satisfaction.
